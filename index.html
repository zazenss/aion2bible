Damage stat value on Global, best to worst

Double Chance > Front/Back Dmg Boost > Weapon Dmg Boost > Damage Boost > Crit Dmg Boost

Global's 50% crit cap weakens Crit Damage Boost. On Korea it ranks about even with Weapon Damage Boost, above Damage Boost.

Flat attack, best to worst

Attack > Max Attack > Attack Bonus > PvE / Boss Attack > conditional

Lost when you transfer gear

Philosopher's Stone line, Manastones/Soulstones, Theostones, Potential lines. Stat lines and enhancement carry over.

First priority

Get 2 key Active Skills to level 20 early. Rings and Arcana are the main sources of extra levels.

What is Skill Index?

It compares your DPS on a boss to the average of players within 20 Combat Power of you. A score of 100 out of 100 (+0%) is the median.

CH 01

Gear & basics

Every piece of gear stacks up to seven layers of stats. Knowing which layers survive a transfer is the single most useful thing to learn early.

Beginner tip

Select any item in game and choose Source to see where it drops.

Item grades

Stat rolls and stones climb through the same grade colors: Gray → Green → Blue → Yellow (Unique) → Orange (Heroic).

Armor

Helmet, Shoulders, Chest, Pants, Gloves, Cloak, Boots. Defensive base stats, plus +Passive Skill lines (never Active). In short: defense, some damage, and passive levels.

Accessories

Offensive and defensive stats, weighted toward damage. Only Rings give Active Skill lines.

  • Necklace, Earrings: basic stats + Passive Skills
  • Rings: basic stats + Active Skills
  • Bracelet: Pantheon stats. First from the Ludra raid.
  • Brooch: basic stats. First from the Muspel raid.

Belt & Amulet

Both come from the level 45 main story. They use their own scrolls instead of Enhance Stones: Noble Belt Enhance Scrolls from clearing Strongholds, and Fierce Battle Amulet Enhance Scrolls from turning in feathers. After +10, Substance Morph each into the next grade, up to Heroic. The Belt is defensive; the Amulet is offensive.

Runes

Runes upgrade by combining two together, need Clash Rune Enhance Scrolls, and are the only gear that is destroyed on failure. Most high-ranked endgame players sit around +7 on both.

CH 03

Ideal stat lines

Which lines to fish for on every slot, listed by how rare they are to roll. The percentage is the approximate roll chance, so the lowest numbers are the hardest to land.

How rerolling works

  • Synch Stones reroll one line. Use the same grade as the gear. A lower-grade stone has only a 10% chance to reroll at all, so never do that.
  • Synch Stones come from Substance Morphing Synch Stone Fragments (mostly from dungeons) or any Unique gear.
  • Soul Codex: Reset (Reset Books) rerolls every line at once.
  • Once you have the best combination, move it to new gear with Transfer Stones. Avoid putting a rare line on the Philosopher's Stone slot, because that slot never transfers.
  • Heroic Transfer Stones are scarce. Moving Unique to Unique is cheap; think hard before transferring into Heroic (see Chapter 5).

CH 04

Skills

Three types: Active, Passive and Stigma. Active and Stigma skills unlock Specialty Perks as they level.

Active skills

Level them to 10 with Wisdom Stones, which come from leveling, turning in feathers, and the Nightmare and Shugo Festival shops. Levels beyond 10 come from gear stat lines, the Daevanion boards and Arcana. You can equip up to three Specialty Perks, unlocked like this:

  1. LV8

    First 3 perk options and slot 1

  2. LV12

    4th perk option and slot 2

  3. LV16

    5th and final perk option

  4. LV20

    Slot 3. The build comes online here.

Every class has key skills that need level 20 before the build works. For Gladiator (Overhead Slam) and Templar (Judgment), a single skill is over 60% of total damage. Endgame Korea runs 2–5 skills at 20 depending on class. Global is likely to launch with fewer Arcana cards, so commit to your two most important skills early and chase them on Ring lines and Arcana.

Passive skills

Upgraded the same way as actives, with no perks. Once your main actives hit 20, push your class's key passives. They carry a lot of damage; endgame Korean players take some to level 25–30.

Stigma skills

Special skills with longer cooldowns. Each has 5 perks, all active once unlocked by level, and you can equip up to 6 (possibly fewer on Global).

  • Levels 1–20: Stigma Shards, made by combining Unstable Stigma Shards from PvE or bought with Abyss Points (Supply Requests, Daily Duties, Weekly Commands, and Abyss farming).
  • Levels 21–25: gold Superior Stigma Shards. You get some leveling from 45 to 50, plus one for each Stigma skill you take to 20.
  • Fully maxed: three skills at 25, one at 23, the rest at 20.

Key Stigmas give big buffs and raise important passives. Supports in particular depend on Stigma levels for skills like Undefeated Mantra and Light of Protection.

CH 05

Enhancement

Upgrading gear: enhancement levels, socket stones, Soul Binding, and transferring everything to a better piece.

Growth

  • Spend Kinah and Enhance Stones up to +15 (Unique) or +20 (Heroic), then Amplify Stones for 5 Amp levels.
  • Amplify Stones are made by Substance Morphing Amplify Fragments with Enhance Stones.
  • The success rate soft-resets to 60% going from +15 to +16.
  • Enhance Stones are the bottleneck even for players who spend money.
Potential Enhance

Potential is a PvP-only stat on Global, so skip it for PvE. On Asia servers it adds PvE Damage Boost/Tolerance using Potential Stones made by Substance Morphing spare gear.

Manastones & Soulstones

Each stone you consume rerolls every socket on the piece. Save Superior stones for endgame gear you will keep, because they are lost on transfer and get expensive.

  • Aim for at least one yellow damage line. The jump from blue (+30) to yellow (+100) is big, and +100 equals 1% on your stat sheet.
  • Two yellow Attack lines are about equal to one yellow Damage Boost line.
  • One yellow Attack (+10) line is a fine placeholder when stones are short.
Front/Back Dmg Boost > Weapon Dmg Boost ≈ Crit Dmg Boost > Damage Boost > Attack

Theostones

One per weapon, and identical stones don't stack. Each has a chance to proc on hit, from crowd control to damage. More Combat Speed means more hits and more value from low-chance stones, though ping affects this. Even the best ones are a tiny share of DPS, so many players just pick the one with the nicest weapon glow.

The Philosopher's Stone line does not transfer.

Transfer

Transfer moves your stat lines and enhancement onto a new piece. It costs Transfer Stones, made from Transfer Stone Fragments. Outside a few shops such as the Season Shop, fragments only come from Substance Morphing Heroic gear, at 5 fragments per piece.

Transfer StoneFragmentsHeroic pieces

  • Same grade to same grade costs 1 Transfer Stone of that grade.
  • Unique to Heroic also needs 5 extra yellow Transfer Stones.
  • Unique fragments work the same way; higher item level gear gives more (x1 to x5).
  • You cannot upgrade a Unique Transfer Stone into a Heroic one.
Kanon's advice

Hoard Heroic fragments early; you will need many later. Skip the first Heroic set (Ancient Spirit) and wait for the second (Faded Shadow) before moving to Heroic. Rolling and transferring on Unique costs almost nothing by comparison.

CH 06

Arcana

A card system that works like a second set of gear: each card type is a slot, and cards belong to sets with set bonuses.

Card types

ParchmentPantheon stat + half of your Active Skills
CompassPantheon stat + the other half of your Active Skills
BellPantheon stat + half of your Passive Skills

There are 7 sets in total. Global launches with fewer; Asia launched with 5 cards (Compass through Mirror) and 2 sets (Primal Vigor, Magic Armor).

Leveling and crafting

  • Transcendence dungeons drop Arcana cards and Training Arcana. Feed either into your equipped card, up to level 5.
  • Each level adds +1 to a random skill line on the card, or a small stat bump if it has no skill lines.
  • Global: feeding a card into another transfers all of its EXP, 1:1, and it carries through if you later feed that card on again. Card type doesn't matter and there is no bonus for feeding the same card. (On Asia servers you lose part of the EXP, and same-type cards give bonus EXP.)
  • Crafting through Transmute lets you choose the lines. It costs Crystals, made by Substance Morphing shards from different Transcendence dungeons. Absolutely worth doing.
  • Chalice and Scale have the largest pools and are the hardest to roll perfectly. If you plan a 4-line craft, craft the Chalice first.
  • Until then, a cheaper set that gets your key Actives to 20 is better than waiting for perfect lines.

CH 07

Daevanion boards

Eight Paragon-style boards (like Diablo 4) where each tile gives stats or skill levels. Respeccing is nearly free.

Gray · basic stats
Green · passive skills or double stats
Blue · active skills or % stats
Orange · major stats, on corners and sides

The four white boards

Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel and Triniel share one point pool and cannot all be maxed.

  1. Take your key Active Skill tiles first to reach levels 12, 16 and especially 20.
  2. Then head for the valuable Orange tiles. You will have enough points for every Active and Passive.
  3. Pick up every gray Attack Bonus tile eventually.

Skip for PvE: Multi-hit Resist (Triniel) and Critical Damage Tolerance (Vaizel). Giving these up is a good way to reach more Attack Bonus tiles.

The four colored boards

These give no skill levels and each has its own separate points.

CH 08

Pet Genus

Five boards (Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian, Special), each with nine stat slots. Raising Insight (the Genus level) opens slots and higher grade rolls.

Analyze rerolls every open slot. Lock a slot to keep it; each lock makes the next roll more expensive. You can save up to 3 Genus presets.

Global steps

  1. Don't lock anything. Analyze until every board reaches level 9. Do the Special board first.
  2. At level 9, use Auto Analyze: pick the BIS stat for slot 4 at Unique grade.
  3. Upgrade slots in this order: 4 → 1, 5 → 6, 9 → 7 → 2, 8.
  4. Rainbow Crystals work on any board if you have plenty.

Global BIS slots

All five boards share the same targets for most slots:

Asia server (KR/TW) approach
  1. Level each board to 10 before locking anything, unless you roll a high yellow or orange BIS stat on slot 4. Waiting gives the best chance at top-grade lines.
  2. At 10, Analyze with nothing locked until slot 4 is BIS on every board. Highest priority: Special (Double Chance) and Varian (Front/Back Damage Boost), then Fera (Weapon Damage Boost) and Cogni (Critical Damage Boost).
  3. A high yellow roll can beat a low orange one. Many players accept lower rolls on a first preset and perfect a second.

Non-Special boards: slots 1, 4, 7 Boss Attack > Crit · slots 3, 9 Double Chance (aim for 2% rolls) · slots 2, 5, 8 Max Attack > Attack Bonus · slot 6 Damage Tolerance.

Special board: slots 3, 9 Double Chance · slot 6 Damage Tolerance · the rest Max Attack > Attack Bonus.

Owned effects

Pets also give passive stats as you unlock and level them, mostly by killing mobs and from pet rewards across content. At Global launch the max level is 3. Prioritize Might (every Cogni mob gives 1) and Precision (every Natura mob gives 1). Use Source to find a mob; if it shows no source, it lives on the other faction's land.

CH 10

Closet

Transmog as long-term power: every skin you unlock adds a percentage stat tied to that skin type.

Weapon skins, for instance, raise the attack of your weapon by a percentage. If your Gauntlet's attack is 500 and your closet weapon bonus is +6.7%, the bonus applies to that 500.

Outfit Damage = [ (Min Attack + Max Attack) ÷ 2 ] × Weapon Outfit Increase % + Pure Attack
CH 11

How stats work

How much damage each stat is really worth, and the full damage formula. Much of this math was worked out with the Korean YouTuber Aion Research Lab (AionLab) and is still being refined.

What 1% of each stat is worth

Measured at current endgame Korea on an ideal build, support buffs included. Diminishing returns mean these values will be higher on Global, where players have fewer stats. Front/Back Damage Boost starts at 0% while Crit Damage starts at 50%, so early points favor Front/Back even more.

Korea numbers

Global values are different. Kanon and AionLab are testing them now and will publish Global numbers soon. Until then, use the Global stat order in the cheat sheet at the top.

Diminishing returns, worked through

Double Chance doubles a hit, so with base damage 1, going from 0% to 1% raises average damage from 1.00 to 1.01: a full 1% gain. Going from 1% to 2% moves you from 1.01 to 1.02, which is only a 0.99% gain, because you were already ahead.

At endgame: 80% Double Chance, minus 30% boss resist, plus 12% from support buffs, leaves 62% effective. Over 100 attacks that is 162 damage. At 63% it is 163. So the extra 1% is worth about 0.617% DPS.

Defensive and hit-check stats

Accuracy vs Block / Parry

Accuracy cancels Block one-to-one. About 100 points ≈ 6–7% chance. Parry caps at 80%, reached at a 1,200 gap: with 2,000 Block against 1,000 Accuracy, the target parries 60–70% of the time.

Critical Hit vs Crit Resist

Same formula and same 80% cap. Skill passives add on top, so if a skill already reaches 80% crit, skip crit-increasing perks for it.

Defense vs Penetration

Damage is reduced by 10% of (Defense − Penetration) in PvE and 1% in PvP. Example: 500 damage, 200 Defense, 100 Pen → 490 damage (499 in PvP). Penetration is low value.

Damage Tolerance & Endurance

Tolerance offsets the matching Damage Boost. Bosses have no Front/Back DB, Weapon DB, Crit or Multi-hit, so tolerance against those is useless in PvE. Endurance is your chance to take half damage; bosses can't penetrate it.

Combat Speed

S-tier: stack as much as you can. It depends heavily on ping and FPS. In testing, a 150–200 ms ping gap made a 15–20% damage difference, with Templar, Brawler and Assassin all affected about equally. Spiritmaster may differ because of pet damage.

The damage formula

Tested extensively and the most complete version available. Everything is confirmed except where Multi-hit sits; it may belong outside Attack Increase.

// 1. Attack A = ( ( Pure Attack × Weapon Dmg Boost ) × Multi-hit × Power Shard ) + Attack Bonus B = A × Attack Increase % + PvE Atk + Boss Atk + Species Atk + Front/Back Atk // 2. Skill and defense C = B × Skill Coefficient + Skill Additional Dmg − ( Defense − Penetration ) × 0.1 // 0.01 in PvP // 3. Multipliers Final = C × ( Dmg Boost + PvE Dmg Boost + Boss Dmg Boost + Species Dmg Boost ) × Critical Dmg Boost × Double × Front/Back Dmg Boost × Skill Specialty
Critical Damage Boost value formula

Let K = Guaranteed Crit Ratio, P = Crit Chance, R = Boss Crit Damage Resistance, D = Crit Damage.

E(D) = (1.5 + D − R) × [ K + (1 − K) × P ] + (1 − K) × (1 − P) Value of +1% = E(D + 1%) ÷ E(D)

What counts as Pure Attack

"Pure Attack" isn't an official stat. It is the label for attack that gets multiplied by Weapon Damage Boost:

  • Weapon, Guard and accessory Attack
  • Attack stat lines
  • Manastone / Soulstone Attack
  • Closet

Aulamus vs Kromede

Which Global weapon to build: the Aulamus dungeon weapon or the extendable Kromede. Kanon ran the numbers with AionLab on his 180k CP Gladiator, so exact results depend on your stats and class.

Short answer

Fully maxed with best-in-slot lines on both, Kromede deals slightly more damage. Aulamus pulls ahead once it has a two-line advantage: its extra Might line plus one more strong line Kromede lacks.

When Aulamus wins

Aulamus is better if it keeps its Might advantage and also has one more of these lines that your Kromede doesn't: Damage Boost (2.8%), Weapon Damage Boost (4%), Combat Speed, or Multi-hit (likely, still being tested).
